# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

### Question 1
Sexual harassment involves any unwelcome or offensive behavior such as inappropriate comments, jokes, messages, gestures, unwanted physical contact, or any action that makes someone feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or disrespected.

---

### Question 2
If I face or witness such behavior, I would not ignore it. I would report it to my mentor or management, support the affected person, and cooperate with any investigation to help maintain a safe and respectful workplace.
