### Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you understand a concept deeply by explaining it in simple words as if teaching someone else.

### Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

1. **Active mode** is when the brain is highly focused on solving a problem or learning something specific. It is used while studying, coding, solving equations, or concentrating deeply on a task.

2. **Diffused mode** happens when the brain is relaxed and not directly focused on the problem. During activities like walking, resting, or showering, the brain connects ideas in the background and helps generate creativity and deeper understanding.

Both modes are important because focused learning helps us understand details, while relaxed thinking helps us connect concepts and solve difficult problems.
