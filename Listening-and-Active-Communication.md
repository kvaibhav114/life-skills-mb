# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

### Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

The following are some important strategies for active listening:

1. Should give full attention to the person speaking instead of thinking about your own response or other distractions.

2. Let the speaker finish their thoughts before responding. Interrupting can break communication and make the speaker feel unheard.

3. Use small phrases such as “I understand,” “Go on,” or “Tell me more” to encourage the other person to continue speaking.

4. Show interest through eye contact, nodding, and attentive posture. Positive body language shows that you are listening carefully.

5. During important discussions or meetings, taking notes helps remember details and reduces misunderstanding later.

6. Repeat the speaker’s idea in your own words to confirm understanding. For example, “So what you mean is…”

7. After listening carefully, we should give a response that shows understanding and respect for the speaker’s message.

## 2. Reflective Listening

### Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

Reflective listening is a communication method where the listener carefully understands the speaker’s message and then repeats or explains it back in their own words. The main points are:

1. **Listen carefully to the speaker**  
   The listener should pay close attention to both words and emotions.

2. **Understand the feelings and meaning**  
   Reflective listening is not only about hearing words but also understanding emotions behind them.

3. **Respond by rephrasing**  
   The listener repeats the message in a simpler form to confirm correct understanding.

4. **Avoid judgment or interruption**  
   The listener should not criticize or immediately give advice while the speaker is talking.

5. **Use empathy**  
   The listener should make the speaker feel understood and respected.

6. **Verify the message**  
   After reflecting the message back, the listener checks whether the understanding is correct.

7. **Help reduce misunderstandings**  
   This technique is useful in business meetings, counseling, teamwork, and technical discussions because it keeps everyone on the same page.

## 3. Reflection

### Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

Some obstacles in my listening process are:

1. Thinking about my reply before the other person finishes speaking  
2. Losing concentration during long conversations  
3. Assuming I already understand the topic without listening carefully  
4. Stress or tiredness affecting focus  

---

### Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?

I can improve my listening by:

1. Giving full attention to the speaker  
2. Keeping my phone away during important conversations  
3. Practicing patience and avoiding interruptions  
4. Taking notes in meetings or technical discussions  
5. Asking questions when something is unclear  

---

## 4. Types of Communication

### Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

I switch to a passive communication style when I want to avoid conflict or arguments. For example, sometimes in group discussions or with seniors, I stay quiet even if I have a different opinion because I do not want to create tension.

---

### Question 6: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

I become aggressive when I am under stress, frustrated, or when I feel someone is being unfair. For example, during deadlines or misunderstandings in teamwork, my tone may become harsh if I lose patience.

---

### Question 7: When do you switch into Passive Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes I become passive aggressive when I feel annoyed but do not express it directly. This can happen when someone repeatedly ignores my suggestions or responsibilities. Instead of openly discussing the issue, I may use sarcasm or become silent.

---

### Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive?

I can make my communication more assertive by following these steps:

1. Speak clearly and confidently without being rude  
2. Express my opinions honestly while respecting others  
3. Maintain calm body language and eye contact  
4. Listen carefully before responding  
5. Use “I” statements such as “I feel” or “I think” instead of blaming others  

Assertive communication helps build healthy relationships, improves teamwork, and reduces misunderstandings.
